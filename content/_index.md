Hi! I'm **Rithin**, I study computer science and engineering at PES University, Bangalore. Right now I'm a research collaborator at Imperial College London, working with [Maksim Anisimov](https://maxanisimov.github.io/) and a research intern at [Lossfunk](https://lossfunk.com).

My work revolves around multiple things: environment design, optimization, world models, interpretability, bioml, and reinforcement learning. Although, they all have a common underlying thread (1) how do models behave when with different kinds of data and optimization (2) how do we make models more trustable (3) how can we make our external components (everything except the model like harness and environments) safer against misaligned models. Apart from my research, I'm a geography geek (avid geoguessr player) and have fun doing debates sometimes.
